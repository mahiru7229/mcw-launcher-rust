# GUI Asset Guidelines

## Icons

- Use one coherent icon family or a consistent custom set.
- Keep optical size, stroke/fill, and alignment consistent.
- Use icons to improve recognition, not to decorate every row or heading.
- Provide accessible labels for icon-only controls.
- Verify legibility at actual rendered size.

## Illustration and brand assets

- Use illustrations when they explain, welcome, or provide meaningful feedback.
- Keep decorative artwork out of dense operational areas unless it supports the task.
- Do not invent official logos or imply endorsement.
- Preserve approved asset proportions and usage rules.

## Backgrounds and effects

Textures, patterns, gradients, blur, and glow are allowed only when they support `DESIGN.md`, preserve contrast, and do not compete with content.

## Asset pipeline and licensing

- Follow the repository's existing asset pipeline.
- Prefer local assets for essential UI.
- Record source and license for third-party assets.
- Optimize image size and scale variants as required by the framework.
- Do not add runtime network dependencies for essential interface assets.

## Missing assets

Use a consistent fallback. Do not leave broken image indicators or substitute random emoji.
